# Code of conduct

LinkPort welcomes contributors and users of every background. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. English and Chinese are both welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and respect different experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not post another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, impersonation, and repeated disruptive behavior.

## Moderation

The maintainer, qianmokano, is responsible for applying this policy fairly. Depending on severity and repetition, moderation may include asking for a change, removing content, a warning, a temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. Good-faith reports and participation must not lead to retaliation.

## Reporting

A public repository and dedicated private conduct-reporting channel have not been established yet. If you already have a private communication channel with the maintainer, use it. Otherwise, wait for the published reporting channel rather than posting personal or sensitive incident details in public. Maintainers must establish and document that channel before inviting a public community; Git commit author addresses are not designated reporting addresses.

Reports should include the context and relevant evidence and will be shared only with people needed to assess and address them. For technical vulnerabilities, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
